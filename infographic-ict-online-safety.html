Digital Citizenship

Stay Safe & Smart Online

6 habits for every learner in our computer room

1

Build strong passwords

Long, mixed letters and numbers. Never share them — not even with friends.

2

Think before you post

Once it’s online it can stay forever. Would you be happy for family to see it?

3

Spot the scam

Strange links, “you won a prize” messages and urgent requests are usually phishing. Don’t click.

4

Protect your privacy

Keep personal details (address, school, location) off public profiles.

5

Be kind — netiquette

Treat people online the way you would face to face. Report cyberbullying, don’t join in.

6

Balance your screen time

Take breaks, protect your sleep, and make time offline too.
