The Toolkit

8 tools I use, and how

Each card shows the tool, a link to its site, how I use it to support my teaching, and a real classroom activity drawn from the CAPS Grade 10–12 curriculum.

Canva for Education

LO + ICT
How I use it

I design CAPS-aligned slides, worksheets and posters, and set learners up to co-create visual projects. It is free for teachers and works on low-spec devices through the browser.

LO • Gr 11 • Democracy & Human RightsLearners design a “Know Your Rights” poster explaining three rights from the Bill of Rights and how they protect young people. They present and vote on the clearest design.
ICT • Gr 10 • Social ImplicationsLearners build an infographic on responsible and ethical technology use (netiquette, privacy, screen balance) to display in the computer room.
canva.com/education
Materials I made▸ LO “Know Your Rights” poster▸ ICT online-safety infographic

Mentimeter

LO leadICT
How I use it

I run live, anonymous polls and word clouds so every learner can respond honestly — especially for sensitive LO topics where learners may not want to speak out loud.

LO • Gr 12 • Development of the Self in SocietyAnonymous word cloud: “One word for how exams make you feel.” We read the shared cloud together, then build a class list of healthy stress-coping strategies.
ICT • Gr 10 • Internet & E-safetyQuick true/false poll on password and phishing myths to check understanding before the practical lesson.
mentimeter.com
Material I made▸ LO exam-stress word cloud

Kahoot!

LO + ICT
How I use it

I use game-based quizzes for fast, fun formative review at the start or end of a topic. The live leaderboard keeps energy high and shows me instantly which concepts to reteach.

LO • Gr 10 • Careers & Career ChoicesA quiz matching careers to study paths and NQF levels, used to open our career-research unit.
ICT • Gr 10 • Systems TechnologiesHardware vs software, input vs output device quiz to consolidate terminology before the test.
kahoot.com
Material I made▸ LO careers quiz (playable)

Padlet

LO + ICT
How I use it

I use shared boards for collaborative brainstorming, curated resource walls and quick exit tickets. Learners post from any device with just a link — no accounts needed.

LO • Gr 12 • Social & Environmental ResponsibilityEach learner posts one local environmental issue plus one realistic action they could take; we group the posts into a class action plan.
ICT • Gr 11 • Solution DevelopmentA shared wall where learners collect and rate the best free coding and design resources they find.
padlet.com
Material I made▸ LO social-issues board

Genially

LO + ICT
How I use it

I create interactive images, clickable diagrams and classroom “escape rooms.” Learners click hotspots to reveal information, which turns a static topic into exploration.

ICT • Gr 10 • Systems TechnologiesAn interactive labelled diagram of a computer: learners click each part to reveal its function, then complete a self-check escape room on internet safety.
LO • Gr 11 • Democracy & Human RightsA clickable timeline of South Africa’s Constitution and Bill of Rights, used for a guided inquiry task.
genially.com
Material I made▸ ICT interactive computer diagram

Scratch

ICT lead
How I use it

I teach the logic of coding with drag-and-drop blocks — no typing syntax to slow beginners down. It runs in the browser and has an offline editor, which suits low-resource classrooms.

ICT • Gr 10–11 • Algorithms & Solution DevelopmentLearners plan an algorithm in plain steps, then build a simple quiz game or animate the water cycle — applying sequence, loops and conditions.
Cross-curricularLearners code an interactive LO awareness mini-game (e.g. a “make the healthy choice” quiz) to share with younger grades.
scratch.mit.edu

Wordwall

LO + ICT
How I use it

I turn one set of terms into several ready-made games (match-up, quiz, random wheel, anagram) in minutes. Great for drilling vocabulary and for quick warm-ups.

ICT • Gr 10 • TerminologyA match-up game pairing ICT terms with definitions (hardware, software, input, output, storage) as a lesson starter.
LO • Gr 11 • CareersA “random wheel” of career fields where each learner names the subjects and skills that field needs.

Life Orientation

Learning activities I built for LO

CAPS-aligned activities for Grades 10–12. Each one lists the learning activity, the classroom activity, and the learner-engagement opportunity it creates.

Who am I? Self-image and goals

GRADE 10 • DEVELOPMENT OF THE SELF IN SOCIETY • TOOL: Book Creator
  • Learning activity: learners explore self-awareness, self-image and their different life roles, then set one short-term and one long-term personal goal using a SMART structure.
  • Classroom activity: each learner builds a short “Me and my life roles” digital book — a page on strengths, a page on values, and a goal page with steps and a timeline.
  • Learner engagement: a gallery walk where learners view each other’s books on tablets and leave one encouraging comment, building a positive class climate.
View the material▸ “My Career Path” workbook (Book Creator)

Know your rights, respect difference

GRADE 11 • DEMOCRACY & HUMAN RIGHTS • TOOLS: Genially + Canva
  • Learning activity: learners investigate the Bill of Rights, forms of discrimination (race, gender, disability) and the value of diversity and nation-building in South Africa.
  • Classroom activity: in groups they explore an interactive Genially timeline of the Constitution, then design a Canva “Know Your Rights” awareness poster for three chosen rights.
  • Learner engagement: a structured debate — “When do rights come with responsibilities?” — where each group defends a position using evidence from their research.
View the material▸ “Know Your Rights” poster (Canva)

Contemporary social issues in my community

GRADE 11 • SOCIAL & ENVIRONMENTAL RESPONSIBILITY • TOOLS: Mentimeter + Padlet
  • Learning activity: learners examine a contemporary social or environmental issue affecting their community (e.g. substance abuse, pollution, unemployment) and possible responses.
  • Classroom activity: an anonymous Mentimeter poll surfaces honest views and myths; learners then post one issue and one realistic action to a shared Padlet, grouped into a class action plan.
  • Learner engagement: small groups adopt one action from the plan and report back the following week on what they did and what they learned.
View the materials▸ Social-issues board (Padlet)▸ Word cloud (Mentimeter)

My career path

GRADE 12 • CAREERS & CAREER CHOICES • TOOLS: Kahoot! + Book Creator
  • Learning activity: learners link study and career fields, NQF levels and admission requirements, and practise decision-making and the application process for further study or work.
  • Classroom activity: a Kahoot! review of career fields and NQF levels opens the unit; learners then build a “My Career Path” digital book researching one career, its requirements and application steps.
  • Learner engagement: a mini careers expo where learners pitch their researched career to peers and answer questions, as planning evidence for their portfolio.
View the materials▸ Careers quiz (Kahoot!)▸ Career workbook (Book Creator)

Information & Communication Technology

Learning activities I built for ICT

CAPS-aligned activities for Grades 10–12, designed to run even in a low-resource classroom. Each lists the learning activity, the classroom activity, and the learner-engagement opportunity.

Inside the computer: systems technologies

GRADE 10 • SYSTEMS TECHNOLOGIES • TOOLS: Genially + Wordwall
  • Learning activity: learners identify hardware and software, input and output devices, and storage, and explain how the parts of a computer work together.
  • Classroom activity: learners explore an interactive Genially diagram of a computer, clicking each part to reveal its function, then play a Wordwall match-up of terms and definitions.
  • Learner engagement: a team “key-word relay” where each learner must define a term before passing to the next — fast, active recall.
View the materials▸ Interactive computer diagram (Genially)▸ Terms match-up (Wordwall)

Safe and ethical online

GRADE 10 • SOCIAL IMPLICATIONS / INTERNET • TOOLS: Mentimeter + Canva
  • Learning activity: learners examine the social implications of technology — privacy, security, netiquette, cyberbullying and responsible use.
  • Classroom activity: a Mentimeter true/false poll checks password and phishing myths; learners then create a Canva infographic on responsible technology use for the computer room.
  • Learner engagement: a “would you click?” scenario round where learners vote and justify their choice on realistic online situations.
View the material▸ Online-safety infographic (Canva)

Thinking in algorithms: intro to coding

GRADE 10–11 • ALGORITHMS & SOLUTION DEVELOPMENT • TOOL: Scratch
  • Learning activity: learners plan an algorithm as a clear sequence of steps, then apply sequence, loops and conditions to solve a problem.
  • Classroom activity: starting from plain-language pseudocode, learners build a simple quiz game or animate the water cycle in Scratch; the offline editor keeps it working without steady internet.
  • Learner engagement: a “swap and improve” round where pairs test each other’s projects and suggest one upgrade — real feedback and iteration.
View the material▸ Scratch coding activity (worksheet)

Working with data: spreadsheets

GRADE 11 • SPREADSHEETS • TOOLS: my Spreadsheets tutorial + Google Sheets
  • Learning activity: learners organise data in rows and columns and use basic formulas (SUM, AVERAGE) and a chart to interpret results — building on my own Spreadsheets tutorial.
  • Classroom activity: learners build a class marks or budget table, calculate totals and averages, and create a chart to visualise the data.
  • Learner engagement: a “read the chart” challenge where learners draw one conclusion from their data and defend it to the group.
