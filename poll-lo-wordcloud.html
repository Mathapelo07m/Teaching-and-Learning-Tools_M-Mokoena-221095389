How it works: learners join on their phones with a code and type one word anonymously. Nobody’s name shows, so answers are honest. The more a word is chosen, the bigger it grows. Below is a sample of what a class result looks like.

“One word for how exams make you feel.”

What we do with the result

  • We read the cloud together — learners see they are not alone in how they feel.
  • We sort the words into “feelings I can manage” and “feelings I need help with.”
  • Together we build a class list of healthy coping strategies.

Healthy coping strategies we add to the board

  • Make a realistic study timetable and break work into small chunks.
  • Sleep, eat and move — the body affects the mind.
  • Breathing and short breaks to reset before studying again.
  • Talk to someone you trust; ask a teacher or counsellor for help.
