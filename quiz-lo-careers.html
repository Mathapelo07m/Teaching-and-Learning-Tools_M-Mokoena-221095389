Q1. A typical university Bachelor’s degree sits at which NQF level?
A Bachelor’s degree is NQF Level 7. Matric is Level 4; a doctorate is Level 10.
Q2. Which subject combination best suits a career in engineering?
Engineering needs Mathematics and Physical Sciences — Maths Literacy is usually not accepted.
Q3. What does “APS” stand for on a university application?
Admission Point Score — points calculated from your matric marks to see if you qualify.
Q4. Where would you find reliable bursary and funding information?
Use official sources like NSFAS and the institution’s financial aid office — never pay an “agent” for free information.
Q5. What makes a career choice a good one for you?
A good choice fits your interests, skills and values and is realistic for your situation.
Q6. What is a sensible first step when applying to study further?
Research requirements early and apply before the closing date — to several options, not just one.
