South African Bill of Rights

Know Your Rights

Three rights that protect young people — and how

Equality

Everyone is equal before the law. Nobody may be unfairly discriminated against because of race, gender, religion or disability.

What it means for me: I can expect fair treatment at school and speak up against unfair treatment of others.

Human dignity

Everyone has the right to be respected and treated with dignity.

What it means for me: bullying, name-calling and humiliation are never “just a joke” — they harm dignity.

Basic education

Everyone has the right to a basic education.

What it means for me: I have a right to learn in a safe environment — and a duty to let others learn too.

Rights come with responsibilities

My rights end where another person’s rights begin. Enjoying my rights means respecting everyone else’s.
